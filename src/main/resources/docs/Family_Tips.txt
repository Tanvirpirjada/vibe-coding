Family & Daily Life Tips from Punjab

1. Morning routine
- Wake up early, drink warm water with lemon
- 10 min walk or surya namaskar

2. Food habits
- Always add ghee in dal/roti for taste & health
- Drink lassi after spicy meal to cool stomach

3. Home remedies
- For cold: ginger + tulsi + honey tea
- For headache: apply warm mustard oil on forehead

4. Money saving tip
- Buy vegetables from local mandi early morning — fresher & cheaper
