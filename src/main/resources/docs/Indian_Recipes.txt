Indian Recipes - Family Style

1. Aloo ki Sabji
- Boil 4 potatoes, peel & cube
- Heat oil, add jeera, hing, haldi
- Add potatoes, salt, spices
- Cook 10 min on low flame

2. Kadhi Pakoda
- Whisk besan + curd + water + spices
- Prepare tadka, add mixture
- Cook 15–20 min
- Add pakodas

3. Rajma Masala
- Soak rajma overnight
- Pressure cook
- Prepare masala
- Simmer and serve
